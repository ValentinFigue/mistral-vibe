STANDARD CONTRACT PLAYBOOK — Acme Corp preferred positions

Payment: Net 30 from invoice date. Net 45 or longer is a deviation requiring approval.

Termination: Mutual termination for material breach with a 30-day cure period is standard.
Immediate termination rights for one party only are a deviation.

Limitation of Liability: Aggregate liability cap at 12 months of fees is standard. An
uncapped liability (other than the confidentiality carve-out) is a deviation. Exclusion of
indirect/consequential damages is standard.

Confidentiality: Survival of 2-3 years is standard. Perpetual survival is a deviation.

Indemnification: Provider indemnifies for third-party IP infringement claims (standard).
Broad, uncapped indemnities are a deviation.

Governing Law: Delaware or New York are acceptable. Any other jurisdiction is a deviation.

Data Protection: A Data Processing Addendum with appropriate safeguards is required for any
agreement involving personal data.
